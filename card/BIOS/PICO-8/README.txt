# PICO-8

slot does not come with PICO-8. Buy it from Lexaloffle
(https://www.lexaloffle.com/pico-8.php) and copy your own copy here.

1. Download the Raspberry Pi build, e.g. pico-8_0.2.6b_raspi.zip.
2. Put the zip in this folder as it is. slot unpacks it the first time you
   play a cart. You can also unzip it here yourself: pico8_dyn and pico8.dat
   need to end up in this folder.
3. Put .p8 and .p8.png carts in Games/PICO8.

After a restart the PICO-8 shelf has a Splore cart for browsing the BBS.
Carts you favourite in Splore are put on the PICO-8 shelf when you leave.
In PICO-8, MENU goes back to slot and POWER turns the screen off.
